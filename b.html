CRTC:

  • The CRTC has a “Discoverability Framework” that aims to “support the availability and visibility of Canadian and Indigenous content” (CRTC)
  • Indigenous content must be prominently displayed and easy to find

The CRTC also has a framework for mandatory funding amounts:

  • Any broadcasters who earn $25 million CAD or more a year in revenue must financially support Indigenous content
    • 25% of annual revenue for traditional broadcasts
    • 15% of annual revenue for online streaming platforms

The influence of media on identity

IndigiNews is an independent Indigenous-run news organization based in Canada.

How does the media text help to contribute to the overall development of Indigenous media? What social impact does this have?

IndigiNews contributes to the development of Indigenous media by establishing sustainable, community-driven digital journalism models centered on cultural safety, self-representation, and training. Socially, this fosters narrative sovereignty and trust by delivering authentic reporting that counters historical stereotypes and directly addresses the information needs of Indigenous communities.

How does/might this media influence identity?

This media influences identity by reinforcing the independence and ferocity that Indigenous peoples have by running their own news outlet, free from any outside tampering.

This media outlet helps Indigenous peoples control what information is spread about them, and they have the ability to shed light on information that may have been covered up by larger media outlets.

Muskrat Magazine

How does the media text help to contribute to the overall development of Indigenous media? What social impact does this have?

Muskrat Magazine contributes to the development of Indigenous media by operating as an independent digital platform that elevates Indigenous arts, culture, and traditional ecological knowledge through creative self-representation. Socially, this builds cultural pride, fosters intergenerational knowledge sharing, and promotes environmental awareness by bridging traditional teachings with contemporary storytelling.

How does/might this media influence identity?

Muskrat Magazine shapes identity by empowering Indigenous peoples to reclaim their narratives through authentic, culturally rooted artistic and environmental storytelling. By operating independently without external censorship, it helps readers foster cultural pride, ground their personal identity in traditional knowledge, and challenge non-Indigenous media misrepresentations.

Efforts being made to promote and fund Indigenous media

  • CRTC regulations, Online Streaming Act, Tax Credits and Government Grants
  • APTN: First National Broadcaster: World’s first national, independent Indigenous television network broadcasting coast-to-coast. Produces and airs documentaries, news, children’s shows, and dramas by and for Indigenous Peoples. Language Preservation, Broadcasts content in multiple Indigenous languages (Inuktitut, Cree, Ojibwe). Impact: APTN transforms the media landscape by reclaiming screen sovereignty and countering mainstream misrepresentation, normalizing Indigenous languages and perspectives on national prime-time television while driving economic growth through consistent employment for Indigenous directors, writers, actors, and technical crews.
  • imagineNATIVE: Global Showcase: World’s largest presenter of Indigenous screen content (film, audio, digital media). Artist Development: Runs year-round labs, commissioning programs, mentorships, and pitch competitions. Industry Advocacy: Commissions sector reports on Indigenous film production and funding standards. Impacts: imagineNATIVE transforms the media landscape by expanding global reach of Indigenous storytelling by connecting grassroots creators with international distributors and providing hybrid accessibility that allows remote Indigenous communities to engage worldwide.
